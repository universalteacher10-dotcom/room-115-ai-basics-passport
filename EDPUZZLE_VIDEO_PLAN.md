# Room 115 AI Basics — Video Sources & Edpuzzle Planning

## Scope
For a high-school ELA elective—not a coding unit. The **existing 25-question AI Basics Certification Test** remains unchanged. Use no more than **20 minutes of badge learning per 57-minute Studio block**, with the rest for daily Studio routines and students' other work. Students subsequently create their own (1) handwritten two-column notes, (2) original flashcards, and (3) third student-chosen study tool from the teacher's source material. Do not assign the whole Code.org course.

## Your original test alignment: what must still be taught directly

The videos help students understand ideas, but **none of these short videos alone guarantees complete coverage** of your test. Point students back to your source [AI Basics Badge Study Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Kp1XnWhoVCiMLsB7iBQgi8D_zyCHysvX9W6grH-cOEE/edit) for:

- Q1–8: chatbot, information retrieval, generative AI, prompt, LLM, ANI/AGI.
- Q9–12: assumptions, prompt engineering, multimodal AI, role-prompting.
- Q13–18: hallucinations, knowledge cutoff, session context, checking facts.
- Q19–20: ELA academic agency and why AI should support but not replace student thinking.
- Q21–25: NotebookLM, creative generation, multimodal input, study partner, identifying a made-up claim.

Especially **ANI versus AGI**, **knowledge cutoff versus session context**, **multimodal AI**, and **NotebookLM** need brief vocabulary teaching through the teacher's provided material rather than adding entire technical courses.

## Edpuzzle setup
Create **two short Edpuzzles maximum**, one for each Studio introduction slot, selecting the video most aligned to that session. Add 2–3 pauses with simple multiple-choice or short-response checks. Use captions, keep clips focused, and use Edpuzzle progress as a formative check only. The student's **original handwritten notes, flashcards, and chosen study product** remain the required badge evidence. Preserve the official 25-question test, 18/25 pass, existing retake and correction rules, and final approval workflow.
