# AI Passport — Badge System Lock (v1)

## Canonical shared assets
The `assets/badges/source/badge-01.svg` through `badge-14.svg` files are the canonical artwork. Always reuse the exact assets in the website, Google Docs, slides, handouts, and any NotebookLM reference imagery. Do not redraw or substitute emoji/plain circles.

## Design identity
Premium holographic collectible **cards**, midnight navy and electric blue/teal, engraved circuit tracery, luminous inner artwork, layered metallic frame, numbered crest and readable titles. The badges form one coherent 14-piece set, each with unique AI learning imagery.

## States
- Locked: subdued dark steel, low cyan edge glow; artwork remains visible.
- Active: vivid electric cyan/teal rim and focus radiance.
- Earned: bright polished blue/teal light, celebratory shine.
- Collection prestige: progressive gold trim and gold radiance as more are earned; the base navy/teal identity is retained.

## Stability contract
1. Treat SVG files as immutable visual source of truth unless the teacher explicitly approves a redesign.
2. Display the same art in cards, detail panels, study resources, and handouts. No emoji or auto-generated icon fallback silently replacing badge art.
3. Preserve module sequencing, progress persistence, assessment rules, Google Classroom routes, accessibility, and reduced-motion compatibility.
4. Test all 14 assets for valid markup and referenced filenames; visually review locked/active/earned/gold states at desktop and Chromebook/mobile widths.
5. Never change production without explicit approval. The preview branch is for visual approval.

## Reuse in teaching materials
For Docs and slides, export or insert the source SVG as PNG while preserving the design. Use badge 14 art on AI Safety and Real-World Risks materials. Do not use separate approximations.
